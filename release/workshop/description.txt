[h1]Real Vehicle Interiors[/h1]
Turn supported vanilla vehicles and optional KI5 campers, buses and trucks into a home on the road. Walk around inside and take your interior and supplies with you.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How it works[/h2]
The roof and upper walls hide when you enter. Move normally and right-click furniture to use it.
[list]
[*] [b]Custom furniture.[/b] Right now the interiors only support custom RV furniture.
[*] [b]Live inside.[/b] Sleep, cook, wash and manage power, water and heating.
[*] [b]Set up your space.[/b] Store supplies, place items and rearrange custom RV furniture.
[*] [b]Make it yours.[/b] Rename your vehicle and paint its interior.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[b]Build 42.21[/b] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url]. No vehicle mods are required.

Supported KI5 vehicle packs are optional. Install only the vehicles you want, together with their own dependencies.

ZombieBuddy loads the Java component for roof cutaways and normal standing and walking animations. [b]Approve it when prompted.[/b]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Compatibility & saves[/h2]
Should now be compatible with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint![/url]. BUT the interiors still only work in the isometric view.

Should be safe to add to or remove from existing saves. [b]Back up first[/b]; custom furniture and stored items may disappear.
