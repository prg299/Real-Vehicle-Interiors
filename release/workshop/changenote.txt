2.0.8 - Feedback fixes, F700 furniture, Shift-running and traits

Fixes from player feedback:
- Walking inside now ends fast-forward, as walking anywhere else does. Walking to furniture by clicking still keeps it, like the game outside.
- Reduces pauses when picking up or placing furniture and cargo boxes, especially in a full F700.
- The F700 box truck takes RV furniture now: clear the boxes along a wall and a kitchenette, washroom, fridge, wardrobe, cabinet, heater or shelf fits there. Boxes and furniture keep out of each other's way. Existing trucks keep their boxes where they are.
- Clears leftover green furniture placement previews.
- Lying down on a bed or getting up no longer flickers through a standing pose on the bed.
- Sends generator battery-charge changes to multiplayer clients more promptly.

Also in 2.0.8:
- Keeps the hood out of the cargo cutaway shading, removing the false dark band.
- Vehicle repair no longer fills deliberately empty cargo slots, preserving varied box-stack heights.
- Existing saved boxes and their contents are preserved. Extra boxes already installed can be picked up normally.
- Adds Shift/Run movement inside interiors and smooths other players moving around inside.
- Clears stale interior animation flags after seat changes and exits.
- Enclosed interiors suppress Agoraphobic outdoor panic and scale Claustrophobic panic to cabin size. MP trait effects require ZombieBuddy's Java loader on the server too.

Known beta limitations:
- Fresh MP bus/save-reload, loose-item right-click and complete trait-suite verification remain pending.
- An occasional server-side standing-passenger position reset with the engine running remains under investigation.
