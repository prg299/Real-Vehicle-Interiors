2.0.12 - F700 furniture, storage and generator fixes

- Adds two full-size F700 furniture spaces for salvaged beds and dinettes, with working furniture seats. Existing cargo-box positions are preserved.
- Repairs storage boxes and fridges stuck at 0 or 1 kg without replacing furniture or stored items.
- Keeps furniture installed in originally empty slots after saving and reloading.
- Generator fuel use now follows appliance load: a fridge-only setup uses 0.2 litres per game hour at the default setting.
- Starting or restarting the generator no longer burns fuel for time it spent switched off.
- Keeps the generator and appliances updating while the vehicle is parked with its engine off.

Multiplayer remains experimental. Back up existing saves before updating.
