2.0.19 - Interior performance fixes

- Fixed severe lag in vehicles with stocked shelves. Unchanged inventories reuse their display layout, and the item display limit now also bounds packing work.
- Fixed interior animal render models being retained after removal or leaving the vehicle. Queued animations finish before their resources are released.
- Removed unnecessary outdoor player-update allocations and repeated loose-item travel speed queries.

Existing vehicle, furniture, loot and utility IDs are unchanged. This update does not require starting a new save.
