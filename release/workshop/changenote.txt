2.0.11 - Bed animation and height fixes

- Uses vanilla bed animations for getting in, falling asleep, waking and getting out instead of snapping directly into the sleeping pose.
- Aligns resting and sleeping poses to the mattress height.
- Shows the normal sleep confirmation before walking to the bed; cancelling leaves the character standing.
- Includes 2.0.10's fixes for weapon aiming crashes and stuck walk-in entry actions.

Dedicated-server gameplay and wider bed-layout verification remain pending; this is a beta update.
