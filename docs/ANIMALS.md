# Animals support — WIP

Branch: `animals-support`. Unreleased and kept separate from `main` and the Workshop mod.

Includes animals inside vehicles, carry/drop/pickup, food and water, and Companion Dogs entry/exit. The original integrated Java/Lua work and seven translation sets are preserved. Current local checkout: `D:/Stuff/Projects/Real-Vehicle-Interiors-Animals`; package: `build/rv/package/RealVehicleInteriors`.

Optional dependency: [Companion Dogs](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3740052292), mod ID `CompanionDogs`.

Animals now initialize their own animation set before the model is constructed and supply the native movement/feeding variables. Satisfied animals wait 20–40 game minutes between decisions, usually stay put, and take shorter steps. Existing saved positions acquire a quiet timer without relocation. Local steps check the shared room/furniture geometry along their full length; inaccessible bowls are skipped.

Companion Dogs care runs through its own upkeep rules while the vehicle is loaded, including when the owner is outside. Unloaded time follows that mod's normal pause rule. Its native Stay, Follow and Inspect controls are available inside; obedience and ownership still apply. Stay prevents voluntary wandering while permitting necessary feeding/drinking. Food checks use CD's hunger accessor, including its saved hunger for armed dogs. Place bowl uses the interior placement cursor regardless of mod load order. Keep food and water in separate stocked CD bowls, or place suitable food/water containers on the floor. The owner's feeding threshold is respected. External Bring removes the old interior occupancy record.

Animal right-click menus now dispatch the game's `OnClickedAnimalForContext` event. The vanilla and Companion Dogs builders supply their normal labels, ordering, icons, tooltips, submenus and availability. `MELens_AnimalContext.lua` adapts pickup and queued world travel to the shared interior walking path, restores temporary routing APIs before walking, and preserves native command/inventory validation. Native petting uses cabin distance on both client and server. Menu building briefly borrows the actor's loaded square because vanilla dereferences it for distance; the animal's original square is restored immediately, without adding it to the world.
