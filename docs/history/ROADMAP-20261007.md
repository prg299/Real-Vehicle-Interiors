# ME: LENS: roadmap to the full mod

**Next:** publish 2.0.0 (built as source on 2026-10-03, see [PLAN-2.0.md](PLAN-2.0.md) section 0
and "Done so far" below): one more look at the four headings of all eight vehicles on the owner's
machine, then the upload (SteamCMD, the owner logs in).

Decided with the project owner (29 September 2026):

| Question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Furniture placement | Designed **spots** per camper for furniture and appliances; **free placement** for small decor (plushies and other items). |
| KI5's own interior | **An empty shell inside**: in the camper you are in, KI5's interior model is hidden and the camper is furnished only with ME: LENS RV furniture. |
| Outside | **Exactly KI5's model**: ME: LENS' inside (shell and furniture) is only drawn in the camper you are in, so from outside nothing of it shows. |
| Using the inside | **Like any other room**: right-click a piece of furniture for what it does, storage shows in the loot window when you stand at it. No general camper menus; one **Camper Dashboard** (the manager, like VIR's) for the generator, lights and water. |
| Vehicles | The **six KI5 campers** first (Flying Cloud 22, Airflyte, Astrodome, Bambi 16, Scamp 13, Scamp 16). Vans and trucks later. |
| Buses (decided later) | **Seats as furniture**: walk the aisle and sit in the bus seats; the seat rows are ME: LENS furniture you can pick up and swap for beds or a kitchen. The driver's seat stays the vehicle's own. |
| Trucks (decided later) | The **F700 box truck**, full of **boxes of loot** (all one size, stacked, each its own container) inside instead of looting the cargo space from outside. KI5's cargo trailers are **too small to walk into** and are not supported (an earlier plan had them). |
| Inside like outside (decided later) | Dropped items stay on the vehicle's **Floor** and never end up outside; what the game allows on foot works inside (exercise, crafting, reading...). |
| Placing furniture (decided later) | **Place furniture** on an RV furniture item in the inventory: every place it fits lights up, the one under the mouse shows a see-through preview of the piece. |
| Multiplayer | **From the start**: every feature is built server-authoritative and synced. |
| 3D models | Made in **Blender** by script (`tools-src/blender`); no downloaded assets. |
| Art | Inventory icons and the painted material textures are **made by an image model** in the game's style (`tools-src/art/generate.ps1`). **The models look like the game's own furniture sprites** (measured next to them in the game): painted textures in muted, dirty colours, tops lit and sides darker, grime low down, edges catching the light and ambient occlusion in the seams. A flat-colour look (after the Viewpoint packs) was tried on 1 October 2026 and dropped the same day: the project owner found the pieces read as bright blocks. |

Goal: interiors work like any other interior in the game. You loot cabinets and use the appliances through the normal loot window and right-click menus. You walk around, sit, sleep, and leave through the door.

## Building blocks

- **Inside shell** (per camper, Blender): floor, inside wall panels, wheel-well boxes and the utility panel, fitted to KI5's shell (measured from their model, not copied). It hangs on a vehicle part, so it moves with the camper, and it is cut away with the roof.
- **Spots** (per camper): vehicle parts appended to KI5's script at runtime, one per spot. A spot holds one furniture item. Installing it shows its model, adds its container, and blocks its floor area on the walk grid. Vanilla syncs part items, part modData and containers in multiplayer.
- **RV furniture items**: compact RV-sized pieces (bed, dinette, kitchenette with stove, oven and sink, fridge, cabinet, wardrobe, washroom, heater). Placed and picked up from inside the camper.
- **Appliances**, driven by vehicle part Lua (server side in multiplayer):
  - fridge: cools its storage while powered;
  - stove and oven: the oven's container heats, so cooking works as vanilla;
  - sink (kitchen, washroom): water tank, drink, fill containers, wash;
  - heater: warms the passenger compartment like a car heater;
  - lights: the camper's battery.
- **Power and water**: a petrol generator (powers the appliances, charges the battery, makes noise) and a water tank refilled from containers. The Camper Dashboard shows and runs them, like VIR's dashboard.
- **Loot**: new campers come furnished with a default layout; furniture containers get distributions like vanilla kitchens and bedrooms.
- **Decor**: place any item with a 3D model anywhere inside, with a ghost preview; stored in the camper and drawn by the Java part; picked up by right-click.
- **Multiplayer**:
  - several standing positions per camper;
  - walking position and facing synced;
  - each client cuts and lights the camper it is in;
  - appliance and decor state kept in part modData and synced.

## Phases

1. **Foundations**: profiles for all six campers (door, exit marker, cut heights, lamps, floor plans, spots); inside shells; several standing positions and walking sync for multiplayer; KI5 storage stays reachable while it still holds items, so old saves lose nothing.
2. **Furniture**: more pieces (bunks, sofa, overhead cabinets, shower), loot distributions, crafting.
3. **Utilities**: propane from KI5's tanks for the stove and heater, shore power from a nearby generator.
4. **Decor placement**: free placement of 3D items (plushies and so on).
5. **Polish**: controller walking, sandbox options, translations, Workshop page, documentation.

  - Dropping (menu or drag) goes to the Floor inside; exercising works inside.
  - **Nothing of KI5's pokes through the inside** (found by looking at every vehicle from all four headings, enlarged, not by one capture): window glass is hidden, the walls have no window openings, the body is not drawn inside each window's box and a bus's window armor is hidden, so frame rails, pillars and bars no longer stand on wall tops; a door leaf stays, painted like the room inside and on its edge; the wheel-well boxes on the camera's side are cut to the floor; the tips of a trailer tongue's arms and a door frame's jamb faces that stand inside the lining are not drawn (`clipBody`: only faces that do not look down, so the hull's own skin stays).

  - **Floors and wheel wells stay inside the hull**: where KI5's hull tucks in under the floor (the Scamp 13 and 16, the Airflyte) the floor followed an outline taken 0.30 above it and showed as a strip of planks under the black skin, and the wheel wells' end plates stuck out of the arches across the tyre. The floor now follows the hull's inside at floor height with the wall sloping up from it, the plates stop at the hull's line, the near-side wheel wells draw nothing below the floor (their liner showed through the arch beside the tyre), and the Scamp 13's bed and dinette stand a little further in; `dev/floor_overhang.py` and `dev/piece_overhang.py` measure it.

  - **Zombies no longer show inside the walls**: a zombie banging on the far wall was drawn in front of the whole camper (the game sorts a character by its square), so its head, arms and legs showed over the cut wall, inside the camper. Changes the Java jar (one ZombieBuddy approval).

  - **Faster rounds**: `dev/iterate.ps1` does one round in one command and one picture; `rv_kit.py` bakes only the pieces that changed (`build/rv/bake-cache.json`, about 50 s instead of about 3 minutes when nothing did) and a retry after Blender's crash goes on from the pieces already baked.

  - **Boxes**: every box is the same plain model (no labels, stencils or stamps), and the truck's stacks differ in height: one to three boxes high to begin with (`heights` in the spec), 63 places in all (46 boxes), the rest bare so a stack can be built up to three.   - **Boxes do not float**: a box with nothing under it falls to the lowest free place of its stack (`settleBox`).
